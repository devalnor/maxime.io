Preserve identity, facial geometry, expression, front-facing pose, hair, eyes and photographic texture faithfully. No beautification, no invented face, no stylization, no particles. Output square 1024x1024 monochrome photograph: isolated head on perfectly solid pure black #000000 background. Keep original aspect ratio of the head (do not stretch), centered horizontally, hair top at 4% and beard bottom at 96%, generous black space to both sides. Clean natural silhouette around hair and jaw; no neck visible, no glow, no text. This is an editing/cutout task, not a new portrait.
